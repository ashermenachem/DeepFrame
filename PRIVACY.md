# DeepFrame Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 — Effective September 4, 2026**

DeepFrame requires an account to use photo tools. It stores account details, uploaded originals, generated reports, plan usage, and material product and security actions to provide cross-device history, enforce limits, and respond to abuse. It does not sell personal information, collect payment-card numbers, or receive passwords from Google, GitHub, or an email provider.

The complete policy is maintained in the application at [deepframesearch.vercel.app/privacy](https://deepframesearch.vercel.app/privacy) and in [`lib/legal.ts`](./lib/legal.ts).

Key points:

- Users can access only their own profile and history; profiles are not public or searchable.
- Uploaded originals, cleaned copies, and reports remain in private account history while the account is active.
- Material activity and security records are automatically removed after 180 days, subject to documented security and legal exceptions.
- Hiding a history item archives it from the account view; it is not represented as permanent deletion.
- Users can submit a separate permanent account-deletion request.
- Authorized administrators may review account information only for security, safety, abuse, support, or legal reasons, and administrative changes are logged.

This policy is a product disclosure and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial launch.
